# Run52 Claim Boundary

## Safe claims

- Run52 ingests 32/32 teacher-validated Run51 stricter constrained N24/N40 batch32 cases.
- Run52 builds native combined328 with N12=36, N16=36, N24=128, N40=128.
- Run52 builds combined328_plus_N32 with N12=36, N16=36, N24=128, N32=332, N40=128.
- Run52 evaluates whether stricter penalty-guard candidate selection improved native Stage 3 teacher metrics.
- Run51 is teacher validation of native N24/N40 stricter constrained candidates, not N32 cases.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim N32 itself was newly teacher-validated in Run51.
- Do not claim N32 caused Run51 improvements.
- Do not claim GNN-RL superiority.
- Do not claim online RL.
- Do not claim arbitrary-N generalization.
- Do not claim physical optimum.
- Do not claim solver/ODB extraction happened in Run52.
