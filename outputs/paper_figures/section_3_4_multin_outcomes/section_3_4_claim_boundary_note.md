# Section 3.4 Claim Boundary Note

Paper-safe wording:

- The framework identified teacher-validated high-performing scan orders across tested native N values.
- Results are bounded to N12/N16/N24/N40 and the tested teacher-labelled strategy space.
- All comparisons use within-N rankings or objective-specific leaderboard views.
- N32, if mentioned, is auxiliary fixed-N32 context and is not merged into native multi-N outcomes.

Avoid these claims:

- global optimum
- all scan strategies
- arbitrary-N generalisation
- online RL control
- physical experiment validation
- beating every possible known or unknown scan strategy
